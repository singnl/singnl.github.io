FSAE Fuel System

E85 fuel system design for Purdue FSAE's PF25 endurance race car

Overview

Designed the fuel system for Purdue FSAE's PF25 race car to accommodate E85 fuel for endurance events, replacing the previous 93 octane setup. Modeled in Siemens NX, the system integrates a Yamaha R6 fuel rail with Bosch injectors, a Daschewerk fuel pump, Holley HydraMat, an EFI fuel pressure regulator, and a fuel cell with custom internal baffles and chassis mounts.

Capacity Calculation

Fuel capacity was derived from MoTeC i2 telemetry recorded during PF24's Michigan endurance run, which consumed approximately 5L on 93 octane. Ethanol carries roughly 33% less energy content than gasoline (per the U.S. Energy Information Administration), so the equivalent E85 volume, plus a 10% safety margin, set the target tank size.

Derivation

PF24 Michigan endurance consumption (93 octane) 5.00 L
E85 energy-content adjustment (× 1.33) 6.65 L
10% safety margin 7.315 L target
Final tank capacity (as designed) ≈ 7.65 L

Tank size and location were optimized around this target for regulation compliance within the PF25 chassis.

System Components

Rail Yamaha R6 fuel rail paired with Bosch fuel injectors
Pump Daschewerk fuel pump
Pickup Holley HydraMat fuel pickup for low-level and cornering reliability
Regulator EFI fuel pressure regulator
Cell Custom fuel cell internal baffles and chassis mounts, sized to 7.65 L

Outcome

The redesigned system successfully transitioned PF25 to E85 while meeting endurance fuel demand with margin to spare, and the tank envelope fit within the regulation and chassis packaging constraints carried over from PF24.
